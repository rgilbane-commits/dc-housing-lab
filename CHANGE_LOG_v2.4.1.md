# v2.4.1 - Citywide Geographic Coverage

- Added representatives for Wards 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7. Retained Wards 1, 3 and 8.
- Added reference geographies for all ten official Comprehensive Plan planning areas, including Near Northwest and Lower Anacostia Waterfront/Near Southwest.
- Expanded map, GIS, ACS and Census approximate denominator selectors and ten-area comparison loops.
- Missing historical baseline quantities for newly added areas are explicitly **Not verified**, never zero or invented.
- Finance presets for new neighborhood references are explicitly illustrative.
- **Important:** this is all-planning-area coverage, not ward-level aggregation. Ward labels are reference locations, not official ward geometries. Actual ward-level housing statistics require separate ward polygons and geographic assignment.
- Existing public investment allocation engine remains limited to its original three planning-area strategies; it is not described as a citywide allocator.
